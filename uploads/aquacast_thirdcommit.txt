# 1. CORE PRODUCT DIRECTION

AquaCast should no longer feel like merely:

> "A groundwater prediction + notification system."

The prototype should communicate this product:

> **AquaCast — AI-powered Climate-Smart Farm Digital Twin**

Core idea:

> **Forecast → Simulate → Optimize → Finance → Verify**

AquaCast should help a farmer understand:

1. What is happening to their groundwater?
2. How much water will their crops require?
3. What climate/weather risks are coming?
4. Which crops/crop combinations are better suited?
5. What happens if they change their farming practices?
6. How much water can they save?
7. How much income can they retain/gain?
8. How much CO₂e can they avoid?
9. What can they do with crop residue?
10. What climate intervention should they adopt?
11. How much would that intervention cost?
12. Could it be financed?
13. Did the predicted impact actually happen?

The final prototype should feel like a **climate intelligence and decision-support platform**, not an alert dashboard.
